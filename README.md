# Lineup Autopilot

An easier way to set daily lineups in our Fantrax league (Adam Silver Fan Club). Rank your players once; Quick Lineup
fills each day's legal lineup and plans the 48-game weekly cap; tap to move players; press Send and it lands in Fantrax.
Optional autopilot sends your Quick Lineup each morning if you haven't.

Sign in with your Fantrax login. The app signs in to Fantrax as you once and keeps only that session; your password is
encrypted in your browser and never stored. It can only change who starts and who sits on your own team. It never
adds, drops or trades players.

This repo is the page only (static, GitHub Pages). `solver.js` and `plan.js` are copies from the private jobs repo,
where the background jobs and server functions live.
