Lineup Autopilot

Your Fantrax lineup, set in one tap.

Sign in with your Fantrax login

Lineup Autopilot sets your Fantrax lineup for you. It signs in to Fantrax as you, once, and keeps only that session, the same way Fantrax's own "remember me" does. Your password is encrypted on this phone and never stored.

It can only change your own lineup: who starts and who sits. It never adds, drops or trades players.
